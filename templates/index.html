About House of Hamill

House of Hamill owes its existence — almost entirely — to a series of cancelled flights. The trio formed when Rose Baldino and Brian Buchanan (formerly of Canadian Celtic rock band Enter the Haggis) first performed together at the 2014 Folk Alliance International conference after bandmates couldn't fly in due to weather. Years later, when hired musicians' flights were cancelled for a Colorado festival, local bassist Caroline Browning stepped in — and the chemistry was undeniable.

Now based in Pennsylvania, House of Hamill has become a fixture on U.S. festival stages. Their original composition "Banks of the Brandywine" won the Grand Prize in the 2024 John Lennon Songwriting Contest (Folk category), and their album "Wildfire" hit #1 on the FAI Folk Charts.

Our Food Drive Mission

At every show, we collect non-perishable food items for local food banks and pantries. With your help as a volunteer, we can ensure every performance makes a meaningful impact on families facing food insecurity.
