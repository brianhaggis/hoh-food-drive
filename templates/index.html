Fighting Hunger, One Show at a Time

1 in 8 Americans faces food insecurity. That's over 44 million people — including 13 million children — who don't know where their next meal will come from. We believe live music can be a force for change.

At every House of Hamill show, we collect non-perishable food items for local food banks and pantries. With volunteer help at each venue, we ensure donations go directly to families in that community who need them most.

Volunteer & Get Two Free Tickets

We need one volunteer per show to set up the donation station beforehand, then collect and deliver the food afterward. During the concert? You're in the audience enjoying the show with your free tickets!

It's a small time commitment with a big community impact.

About House of Hamill

House of Hamill is a Pennsylvania-based Celtic-Americana trio born from serendipity. When cancelled flights at the 2014 Folk Alliance conference brought fiddler Rose Baldino and multi-instrumentalist Brian Buchanan together, their chemistry was instant. Years later, another flight cancellation introduced bassist Caroline Browning—and the trio clicked. Now festival favorites nationwide, they won the 2024 John Lennon Songwriting Contest Grand Prize (Folk) for "Banks of the Brandywine," while their violin cover of "Sweet Child O' Mine" has amassed over 16 million views. Their album Wildfire topped the FAI Folk Charts in August 2024.
